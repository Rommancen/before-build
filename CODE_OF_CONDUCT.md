# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Before Build to be a useful, respectful, and evidence-oriented project. Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive;
- discuss ideas rather than people;
- distinguish facts, assumptions, and opinions;
- welcome good-faith questions and correction;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and doxxing.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository owner through [GitHub](https://github.com/Rommancen). Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.
