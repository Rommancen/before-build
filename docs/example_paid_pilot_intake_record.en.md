# Example Paid-Pilot Intake Record

> **Illustrative and anonymized example.** The company, people, amounts, and results are fictional. A paid pilot does not prove repeatable economics or authorize scale automatically.

## Situation

A regional service company loses requests when work moves from its sales team to field operations. The company currently uses email, a shared spreadsheet, and manual follow-up. It agrees to a narrowly scoped paid pilot instead of requesting a full platform.

## Snapshot

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Problem | Handoffs from sales to field operations are missed, causing delayed customer appointments and manual rework. |
| Observable workflow | A confirmed request is copied from email into a spreadsheet, assigned manually, and followed up by phone when a response is late. |
| Existing workaround | Shared spreadsheet, email, and daily supervisor review. |
| Problem owner | Head of field operations, accountable for missed appointments. |
| User | Two dispatch coordinators. |
| Buyer | Operations director, who approves the pilot. |
| Payer | The service company’s operations budget. |
| Economic commitment | A fictional 30-day paid pilot with a signed order form and a small fixed fee. |
| Measurable outcome | Reduce missed handoffs by 50% and reduce manual follow-up time by 20% during the pilot. |
| Review date | Day 30, with a written result review by the buyer. |

## Decision

- **Status:** `BUILD AUTHORIZED` for the limited paid pilot only.
- **Evidence level:** `E3` for this narrow commitment; repeatable economics and scale remain unproven.
- **Allowed now:** configure one workflow, use two coordinators, process one request type, and measure the stated outcomes for 30 days.
- **Not allowed:** build a general platform, add unrelated integrations, expand to other teams, or authorize scale from this pilot alone.
- **Riskiest assumption:** reducing missed handoffs creates enough durable value for the buyer to renew at a sustainable price.
- **Next test:** compare the baseline with pilot results and conduct a renewal/expansion commitment review with the buyer.
- **Kill condition:** stop or reframe if the target reduction is not observed, the workflow requires unacceptable manual effort, or the buyer will not consider a paid continuation at the review date.

## What remains unproven

The pilot does not yet prove repeatable delivery, retention, gross margin, a scalable sales process, or defensibility. Those gates require separate evidence. A successful pilot authorizes only the agreed limited scope; it is not permission to scale.
