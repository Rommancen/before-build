# Launch post

## Short version

Most teams do not fail because they cannot build. They fail because they authorize build before validating the problem, alternatives, and costly customer commitment.

I published **Before Build** — a lightweight, evidence-driven template for deciding what to do with a product or custom-development request before estimation, prototyping, and code.

It helps teams separate:

- a feature request from a real problem;
- operational access from willingness to pay;
- discovery from build authorization;
- urgency from evidence;
- a useful prototype from an unsolicited MVP.

The repository includes a decision engine, intake checklist, reusable record template, and an anonymized example.

GitHub: https://github.com/Rommancen/before-build

Feedback and real-world examples are welcome.
