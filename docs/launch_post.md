# Launch post

## Short version

Most teams do not fail because they cannot build. They fail because they invest in an idea, approve a business plan, or authorize a build before validating the problem, alternatives, and costly customer commitment.

I published **Before Build** — a lightweight, evidence-driven framework for deciding what to do with a startup idea, SaaS concept, business hypothesis, business plan, monetization model, pilot, partnership, product request, or custom-development initiative before investment, estimation, prototyping, and code.

It helps teams separate:

- a feature request from a real problem;
- operational access from willingness to pay;
- discovery from build authorization;
- urgency from evidence;
- a useful prototype from an unsolicited MVP.

The same evidence gate applies whether the initiative is called a startup, SaaS, business plan, investment opportunity, pilot, innovation project, or new revenue channel.

The repository includes a decision engine, intake checklist, reusable record template, and an anonymized example.
